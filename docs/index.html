Your clipboard.
Your Mac.

No Cliphoard account, telemetry or automatic cloud sync.

Clip content, images and search data are encrypted at rest with AES-GCM.

How protection works

Keys use the Secure Enclave where available, with a login Keychain fallback. Small operational fields, including timestamps and pin state, remain readable locally.

You can exclude apps from future capture. Sensitive-content filters are imperfect, so you choose what to keep.

Encrypted export uses AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with at least 600,000 iterations. The passphrase is the only key; there is no recovery or escrow.

Optional model downloads and purchase services use the internet. Clipboard content is not sent with those requests.

Capture & reuseThe everyday clipboard, fully covered.Free6 features+
One shortcut over any app
Open Cliphoard with ⌃⌥⌘V and return a chosen clip to the app you came from.
Copy without pasting
Retrieve a clip to the clipboard, or paste it as plain text with formatting removed.
Text, links, colours, files & images
Keep plain and rich text, recognise bare-domain links and hex colours, and browse image thumbnails.
History on your terms
Start with 200 clips, choose another limit or turn the limit off. Pinned clips are never trimmed.
Delete and clear
Remove individual clips or clear unpinned history when you no longer want it kept.
Screenshots that paste into web apps
Optional PNG compatibility rewrites the clipboard to add a browser-friendly format. It is on by default and can be switched off in Settings; the privacy policy explains its trade-offs.
Find & organiseRemember an idea, a category or a date.Free5 features+
Four text search modes
Search exact words, auto-assigned categories, meaning, or all three combined.
On-device semantic search
Your Mac runs the model. Your clips, queries and search indexes are not uploaded.
Choose your text model
Low uses open-ogma-micro; Normal uses open-ogma-small; High uses all-MiniLM-L6-v2. The direct edition downloads a selected model if it is not installed. Each model keeps its own licence and credit.
Tag baskets that stack
Combine developer, writing, business and everyday vocabularies without replacing the others.
Time filters you can type
Choose today, yesterday, this week or a custom range; queries also accept when:yesterday and explicit date ranges.

Light, dark or your system

Local, private & protectedSpecific safeguards, with clear limits.Free6 features+
No clipboard uploads or telemetry
Clipboard processing stays on your Mac. No advertising, usage telemetry, automatic crash-report upload or cloud sync.
Encrypted clip content
AES-GCM protects stored content, images, thumbnails, source-app names and search indexes. Small operational fields such as timestamps, clip kind and pin state remain readable.
Keys held on your Mac
Secure Enclave-backed key generation is used where available, with a device-only login Keychain copy of the derived key. Other Macs use a random Keychain key. No Touch ID prompt is required.
Sensitive capture controls
Transient or concealed pasteboards are skipped, and you can exclude apps. Quarantined clips remain visible and pastable, but receive no embeddings, tags or recognition. Withheld screenshots are not decoded for analysis.
Private local storage
Owner-only folder and file permissions restrict other ordinary user accounts on the Mac.
Network use, explained
Selected model downloads and purchase/restore services may use the network. Those connections do not carry your clipboard content.
Import & move your historyYour data has a way in—and a way out.Free5 features+
Supported clipboard importers
Paste, Maccy and Flycut have been verified against real installations. Alfred uses its published schema. Six other catalogued managers do not yet have adapters.
Preview, merge and undo imports
See what was found before importing. Each batch is tagged for undo; existing clips are not deleted.
Encrypted export and import
Move history between Macs or editions using one passphrase-protected archive. Both directions are included in Free.
Protected in transit, re-sealed locally
The archive uses AES-256-GCM and a passphrase-derived key. Import re-encrypts content with the destination Mac’s key and rejects truncated, reordered or modified archives.
Your passphrase is the only key
There is no recovery or escrowed key. Keep the passphrase safe and treat the archive as sensitive.
More for visual workOptional image tools, still on your Mac.Pro3 features+
Find pictures by description
Local image embeddings let you search for an image using words.
Read text inside screenshots
Apple Vision recognises image text locally so it can be searched. Turn recognition off in Settings; Forget recognised text removes earlier recognition. Recognition and secret detection are imperfect, so excluding sensitive apps remains important.
Find visually similar clips
Use an image’s local perceptual fingerprint to find related-looking clips.

Encryption protects stored content; it cannot protect an unlocked or compromised Mac. Read the privacy details or inspect the open-source code.
